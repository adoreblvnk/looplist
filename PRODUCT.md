# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Individual resale sellers photographing used consumer electronics or collectibles at a desk. They need to turn visible evidence into an honest, editable marketplace listing quickly. Hackathon judges need to understand the complete live Gemini workflow in a two-minute demonstration without reading implementation notes.

## Product Purpose

LoopList converts three to eight item photos into a structured eBay-compatible listing, exposes uncertainty and visible defects, asks only material follow-up questions, and publishes only after seller approval. Success means a seller can inspect, correct, approve, publish, and independently verify one listing without hidden service fallbacks.

## Brand Personality

Precise, tactile, candid. LoopList should feel like a well-made inspection sheet rather than a generic AI assistant: quiet confidence, visible evidence, and direct operational language.

## Anti-references

- Generic AI chat interfaces, dashboard shells, and feature-card SaaS landing pages
- Decorative gradients, glass, oversized rounded cards, colored branding without semantic purpose, or cream-tinted AI-default surfaces
- Marketplace interfaces that hide defects, uncertainty, adapter status, or the approval boundary
- Developer-console aesthetics that expose raw reasoning, internal guardrails, or infrastructure instead of seller outcomes
- Editorial-magazine styling that makes the task feel slower or less familiar

## Design Principles

- Start with the item: photography and observed evidence lead every decision.
- Make trust inspectable: uncertainty, defects, approval, publication, and verification remain distinct states.
- Keep the two-minute path obvious: one primary action per state and no dashboard detours.
- Separate machine work from seller judgment: Gemini proposes; the seller edits and approves.
- Use restraint to increase signal, not to remove useful feedback.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.2 AA for contrast, focus visibility, keyboard operation, labels, status announcements, and touch targets. Do not rely on color alone for confidence or state. Respect reduced-motion preferences and preserve complete functionality without animation. Use plain US English and descriptive image alternative text.
